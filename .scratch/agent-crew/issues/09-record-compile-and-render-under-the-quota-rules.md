# 09: The producer records, compiles and renders under the quota rules

**What to build:** On a compliant plan the producer spends exactly one synthesis
dispatch, then compiles and renders, ending at a narrated preview MP4. The quota rules
bind it the way they bound the scripted proof agents.

Three behaviours are asserted rather than hoped for. The new-Take budget is absolute:
the crew treats it as a hard limit and never attempts to exceed it. An identical
recording input is never redispatched without an explicit replacement authorisation.
And a paused outcome — whether the budget is exhausted or a replacement is required —
stops the crew and surfaces the decision to the operator, because authorisation stays
human.

Placeholder degradation is accepted visibly rather than fought. A preview containing
placeholders is an honest intermediate state and a successful outcome here; the crew
reports it rather than looping trying to resolve it.

The finished preview and its reports are read back through artifact retrieval, so this
ticket also proves the read-back direction of the client under real artifacts.
