# 13: An unservable Brief produces a Decline

**What to build:** Handed a Brief the catalog cannot serve, the crew emits a structured
Decline naming the unmet editorial need, records no Take, and terminates the Run at
declined with any draft plan preserved. Refusing is correct production behaviour, not a
crash and not a degraded attempt.

This is the third proof scenario, and with scenario configuration already lifted into one
table it should be a row plus its Brief and its assertions rather than a new branch in
existing code.

The Brief is cheap to construct: something the catalog's own guidance already redirects
away from, so the crew reaches the Decline through the published surface rather than
through a special case somebody wired in. The researcher's triage is what recommends it;
the producer is what emits it.

What the assertions check is that the Decline names the *editorial* need that went unmet —
a Decline that merely says no teaches the operator nothing and would let a lazy refusal
pass.

**This is acceptance bar (2).**
