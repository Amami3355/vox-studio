# Spec — The ADK production crew researches, directs, and creates its visual material

Status: ready-for-agent

## Problem Statement

Vox Studio has proved that its Production service can take a VideoPlan through recording,
compilation, rendering, and preview retrieval, locally and in the cloud. It has not yet proved the
product promised by the V1 architecture: an ADK production crew that takes one factual Brief,
researches it with Parallel, turns sourced evidence into a narrative, directs the visual story,
creates the missing visual material, and drives Production to a preview.

The current crew concentrates authoring in one plan author. Its Research Agent has not been built,
its production proof used a handed plan rather than an agent-authored one, and its Asset Resolver
stops at an identity cache, a repository library, and an honest placeholder. Implementing the
Research Agent as an independent appendage would leave the same underlying gap: no crew-level
orchestration, no explicit ownership of intermediate creative artifacts, no durable progress model
for the Studio, and no route from an unresolved `AssetRequirement` to a generated image that a
SceneInstance can actually display.

The deadline also makes the wrong kind of autonomy dangerous. A free-form Director must not decide
the order of irreversible production commands, retry a paid operation, bypass the recording quota,
or treat a plausible generated image as accepted art. Research, narrative judgment, art direction,
visual planning, and image creation benefit from models. Command ordering, persistence, validation,
identity caching, spend authorization, artifact integrity, and failure recovery do not.

The user needs one coherent ADK crew whose specialization is visible in the running product, while
the six rules of the frozen architecture remain true: the agents never read implementation, write
semantic rather than physical time, learn SceneCapabilities from the generated catalog, leave hard
constraints to validation, accept honest degradation, and obtain visual consistency from the design
system rather than unconstrained prompts.

## Solution

Build one ADK-native crew behind a single asynchronous interface. It accepts a Brief and operator
policy, emits an ordered stream of phase events, and terminates with a rendered, declined, paused,
or failed outcome. A deterministic Director owns the workflow and delegates only the judgment-heavy
steps to specialist model agents.

The crew has these roles:

- A **Research Agent** decides what a factual Brief needs to establish, calls Parallel through a
  narrow research tool, and returns a structured Research dossier with source provenance. Declared
  test data and fictional Briefs take an asserted zero-call path.
- A **Narrative Agent** turns the Brief and Research dossier into an editorial angle and ordered
  Beats, each carrying its voice-over text verbatim. It does not select SceneCapabilities.
- An **Art Director Agent** chooses from published design-system roles and produces a constrained
  Visual bible. It does not invent raw colors, fonts, easing values, dimensions, or provider prompts.
- A **Visual Planner workflow** first structures Sections, persistent elements, and
  SceneCapability selections from the compact catalog tier, then fills SceneInstances from the full
  specifications of only the selected capabilities. It uses only the four catalog tools and writes
  no physical time or resolved asset reference.
- A **Plan Repair Agent**, reached only after a structured refusal, repairs the refused portion from
  the published checks and named SceneCapability specifications. It does not become an autonomous
  Quality Agent and does not rewrite a plan that Production accepted.
- An **Image Creator Agent** acts only on unresolved asset work selected by the deterministic Asset
  Resolver. It requests a deterministic prompt, calls an image-generation tool, and returns an
  inspectable candidate. It does not own caching, identity, persistence, acceptance, or `AssetRef`
  status.

The Director is a deterministic ADK workflow agent. Research happens first. Narrative and art
direction then run concurrently because both depend on the same Brief and Research dossier but not
on each other. Visual planning waits for both. Once a VideoPlan is accepted, the Director can run
independent long-running production preparation concurrently where policy allows it, while keeping
the recording and image-generation spend gates explicit. Production commands remain a deterministic
sequence through the existing production-client seam.

The Asset Resolver remains a deep deterministic module. It resolves identity cache, project cache,
repository library, and immediate placeholder before asking the Image Creator to generate anything.
Generated bytes are stored on Production's side and addressed by digest. A generated candidate is
not `ready` until the operator accepts it; until then the Compiled document receives a placeholder.
Generation failure produces `failed`, preserves the placeholder rendering path, and never prevents a
preview.

The first delivery is one tracer-bullet Run: a factual Brief produces a Parallel-backed Research
dossier, sourced Beats, a catalog-authored VideoPlan, at least one generated and accepted image, and
a verified narrated preview. This is the highest acceptance seam. Recorded research, model, image,
voice, and Production adapters make the same path deterministic and free in CI; live provider runs
are deliberate operator actions.

## Implementation Decisions

- **The external module is the crew, with one asynchronous interface.** A caller supplies a Brief
  and operator policy, consumes ordered phase events, and receives one terminal outcome. The
  interface does not expose individual agent runners, prompts, provider clients, paths, or
  production transports.
- **The primary acceptance seam is the whole crew.** A factual Brief must cross Research,
  narrative, art direction, visual planning, asset resolution, Production, and artifact retrieval.
  Agent-level tests exist to make failures local, but they do not replace this seam.
- **The Director is deterministic ADK orchestration, not an unconstrained `LlmAgent`.** It owns the
  state machine, dependency ordering, authorization gates, concurrency joins, recovery policy, and
  terminal outcome. Editorial ambiguity is delegated to specialists; irreversible sequencing is
  not.
- **ADK-native asynchronous execution is required.** The crew uses the framework's asynchronous
  runner and event stream. A synchronous compatibility adapter may remain for the existing CLI, but
  no hosted caller is driven through nested event-loop shims.
- **The ADK session implementation is configurable.** In-memory sessions remain the test and local
  default. A durable implementation can be supplied by the hosted crew without changing any agent.
- **ADK session state owns creative workflow state, not Production authority.** It may hold the
  Brief, Research dossier, narrative, Visual bible, VideoPlan, provider job handles, and published
  artifact descriptors. The Run checkpoint and Run ledger remain authoritative for Production
  stage, receipts, recording quota, and artifact bindings.
- **Specialist outputs use distinct state keys and validated contracts.** A phase cannot advance on
  malformed output. Large binary data never enters state; state carries handles, metadata, and
  digests.
- **Research is one agent with one provider-neutral tool.** The Research Agent plans the inquiry and
  interprets results. The research interface hides authentication, request formatting, polling,
  retry behavior, and Parallel-specific response shapes.
- **The research interface has two adapters from its first commit.** The live adapter uses Parallel
  only during explicitly live runs. The recorded adapter is the default for tests and deterministic
  rehearsals. Crew code never branches on which adapter it holds.
- **Research output is a Research dossier, not loose prose.** It carries stable source identifiers,
  sourced claims, sourced statistics, sourced quotations, contradictions or uncertainty, and visual
  opportunities. Downstream roles cite identifiers rather than copying source locations.
- **The no-research path is decided before the research tool is reachable.** A Brief declared as
  test data or fiction produces an observed call count of zero. The Research Agent is not asked to
  make a call and then discard it.
- **Catalog serviceability is not assigned to the Research Agent.** Research can report missing or
  contradictory evidence. Whether available SceneCapabilities can express the Brief is decided by
  visual planning and validation; the Director turns that evidence into a Decline when appropriate.
  This narrows the older researcher ticket's mixed responsibility deliberately.
- **Narrative and art direction are separate model roles.** Both follow Research and may execute in
  an ADK parallel workflow. The Narrative Agent owns angle and Beats. The Art Director owns a
  constrained Visual bible. Neither selects or fills SceneInstances.
- **The Research dossier remains a sidecar to the VideoPlan.** Beat-to-claim provenance is retained
  in the crew's project state and evidence rather than adding implementation or source locations to
  the frozen VideoPlan shape. A later product decision may publish provenance in the video document,
  but this spec does not make the compiler consume it.
- **The Visual bible selects closed design-system vocabulary.** Theme, motion intent, semantic color
  roles, treatment families, and visual motifs are selected from published values. Raw colors,
  fonts, easing parameters, frame counts, dimensions, and full provider prompts are not agent output.
- **The Visual Planner is an ADK subworkflow with two roles.** The Visual Structurer reads the
  complete compact selection tier and writes Beats-to-Sections structure, persistent elements, and
  SceneCapability selections. The Scene Author reads the full specifications of exactly those
  selected SceneCapabilities and fills SceneInstances. Together they produce one VideoPlan.
- **Catalog tiers are generated projections.** Tier membership is published by the contract. Values
  are byte-identical selections of canonical catalog material, never summaries or crew-maintained
  lists. An unavailable selected capability is a finding rather than permission to hand the Scene
  Author the entire catalog.
- **The role split changes ADR-0017 deliberately.** Before role-specific catalog projections land, a
  successor ADR must supersede the whole-catalog-author half of ADR-0017, argue the change on the
  measured capability wall rather than cost alone, and name every prefix measurement it retires.
  Prefix identity remains a per-role invariant.
- **The existing plan-author interface remains a compatibility seam.** Handed and scripted authors
  continue to drive deterministic Runs. The ADK Visual Planner may satisfy that seam through an
  adapter, but the rest of the crew does not receive a Production client, path, root, directory, or
  file through it.
- **Tools crossing an author seam obey ADR-0016.** They are explicitly offered, callable without
  location knowledge, leak-scanned as prompt material, available to keyless construction tests, and
  incapable of reaching Production sideways.
- **The Visual Planner has exactly four tool capabilities.** It can search SceneCapabilities, read a
  selected specification, validate a SceneInstance, and validate a VideoPlan. Research, image
  generation, persistence, recording, and rendering are not available to it.
- **Plan repair is conditional and bounded.** The repair role receives the refused plan, structured
  findings, published check meanings, and the full specifications of only the implicated
  SceneCapabilities. It holds accepted structure fixed where the refusal does not require changing
  it. Exhausting the repair budget stops or Declines; it never triggers an unbounded loop.
- **The Asset Resolver remains deterministic and retains one shared identity cache.** SceneInstances
  and persistent elements resolve through the same resolver instance. Cache, repository library,
  placeholder, candidate, acceptance, and failure decisions do not belong to a model.
- **Compiler `ASSET_PLACEHOLDER` findings remain the worklist.** There is no second manually
  maintained list of visual work. The Director presents the unique unresolved requirements from
  those findings to the resolver.
- **The Image Creator is an agent paired with a tool.** The agent interprets one bounded unresolved
  visual task and may request generation. The tool owns provider access, credentials, prompt
  derivation, job lifecycle, budget accounting, binary validation, storage, and digesting.
- **Image prompts remain deterministic.** The full prompt is derived from the semantic
  `AssetRequirement`, target aspect ratio, selected closed treatment and theme vocabulary, and the
  design-system palette. The Image Creator cannot add unconstrained style prose. This retains the
  core of ADR-0005 while making generation an agent-visible workflow phase.
- **`identityKey` precedes wording.** One identity produces one generation job and one accepted asset
  per project. A recurring character without an `identityKey` is a VideoPlan finding, not permission
  for the generator to guess equivalence.
- **A generated candidate is not an `AssetRef`.** Candidate metadata and its artifact handle remain
  in crew/Production state. Operator acceptance promotes exact digested bytes to a `ready` AssetRef;
  pending or rejected candidates leave `placeholder`; terminal provider or verification failures
  produce `failed`.
- **Generated bytes live with Production.** The live tool executes on the trusted side or through a
  new published payload-shaped Production command, so the crew needs neither a filesystem location
  nor a privileged upload channel. The Production interface returns job state, candidate metadata,
  descriptors, and sanitized errors.
- **Per-Run generated assets extend ADR-0005 deliberately.** A successor ADR must distinguish a
  generated candidate stored and evidenced inside one Run from an asset promoted into the canonical
  repository library. Canonical promotion still requires human acceptance and a committed file.
  A Run-specific accepted asset is reproducible through its evidence bundle and digest rather than
  through a fresh clone. Generation never occurs implicitly inside Remotion rendering.
- **Image generation is long-running and resumable.** Starting a job and observing its completion
  are distinct states. A dropped caller can recover by job identifier; it does not start another
  generation unless policy explicitly authorizes a replacement attempt.
- **Spend authorization covers voice and images separately.** Before-tool policy gates refuse
  recording or image generation without the corresponding operator grant. Research calls are also
  metered and visible even when they do not share the irreversible recording quota.
- **ADK parallelism is used only for independent work.** Narrative and art direction can run in
  parallel after Research. Independent asset jobs can run concurrently under a deterministic
  coordinator. Research must precede grounded narrative, visual structure must precede image
  generation, and compile must not claim a candidate that has not been accepted.
- **Production remains a deterministic module.** Validate, Preflight, record, compile, render,
  status, and artifact retrieval are invoked by workflow code through the existing production-client
  seam. No model agent receives unrestricted access to those commands.
- **No unsafe automatic retry is introduced.** Read-only status, polling, and digest-verified
  retrieval may retry under bounded policy. Research, image generation, recording, and render start
  are never blindly retried because the first request may have completed after the connection failed.
- **Progress events are public outcomes, not model transcripts.** Events name the role, phase,
  status, counts, safe summaries, and artifact descriptors needed by the Studio. They contain no
  chain of thought, credential, provider payload not intended for users, repository location, or
  implementation detail.
- **Evidence expands with the crew.** The bundle records role names, validated outputs or their
  digests, tool names and safe arguments, provider mode, call counts, phase transitions,
  authorization decisions, repairs, source provenance, image candidates and acceptance, Production
  envelopes, and final artifact verification.
- **The frozen architecture document is not edited.** This feature is an implementation of its ADK
  and Asset Resolver stages plus a deliberate evolution of later ADR decisions. Architectural
  changes are recorded in successor ADRs and the architecture-evolution record.
- **This spec absorbs the older standalone Research Agent ticket.** It also incorporates the async
  requirement and the measured Visual Planner role split. Existing tickets are not implemented in
  parallel as competing architectures; they are closed, superseded, or narrowed when this effort is
  decomposed into implementation tickets.

## Testing Decisions

- **Tests observe module interfaces, not ADK internals.** A test asserts the crew's phase events,
  terminal outcome, provider calls, Production envelopes, and artifacts. It does not assert private
  agent-tree fields, framework object identities, prompt assembly helpers, or the number of internal
  coroutine frames.
- **The highest test drives the complete tracer bullet.** With recorded adapters, one factual Brief
  yields a sourced Research dossier, narrative Beats, a validated VideoPlan, one accepted generated
  asset, a Compiled document, and a digest-verified preview. This test uses the same crew interface a
  Web caller will use.
- **A second whole-crew test exercises honest degradation.** Image generation fails, the affected
  requirement becomes `failed`, the compiler reports `ASSET_PLACEHOLDER`, and the preview still
  renders the editorial placeholder.
- **A third whole-crew test exercises Decline.** An unservable Brief produces a structured Decline,
  no Take, no image-generation spend, and a complete evidence bundle.
- **Default tests use no network, provider credential, synthesis quota, or cloud service.** Research,
  model authoring, image generation, voice, and Production use recorded or in-memory adapters. A
  network sentinel fails any accidental egress.
- **Provider adapters have contract suites.** Live and recorded research adapters return the same
  Research dossier contract. Live and recorded image adapters return the same job/candidate
  contract. A provider-specific field crossing either interface fails the contract test.
- **The Research Agent is tested through observable calls and dossier output.** A factual Brief
  makes the expected bounded research calls and every factual item cites an existing source. A
  declared test-data Brief makes exactly zero calls. Missing or contradictory evidence remains
  present rather than being silently reconciled.
- **Narrative grounding is asserted.** Every factual Beat maps to one or more dossier claim IDs, no
  unknown ID is accepted, and no separate script artifact appears.
- **Art-direction constraints are asserted.** The Visual bible contains only published roles and
  treatments. Raw style values and free-form provider prompts are rejected.
- **Catalog tier tests follow the prior measured design.** Every tier is a byte-identical selection
  of the published catalog, the tiers plus remainder reconstruct each capability, and tier
  membership comes from the contract rather than a crew list.
- **Role authority is asserted structurally.** The Visual Structurer cannot fill props; the Scene
  Author cannot add a SceneInstance or select an unprovided SceneCapability; the Visual Planner has
  exactly the four catalog tools; no author receives a Production client or location-bearing value.
- **Repair tests begin from a structured refusal.** They assert that only implicated material is
  offered, accepted structure is retained where possible, and the bounded loop stops visibly when it
  cannot converge.
- **Prompt derivation is tested as pure behavior.** Equal requirements, target shapes, and visual
  roles produce byte-identical generation requests. Changing `identityKey` alone changes identity
  handling but not the visual subject. The agent cannot append arbitrary style instructions.
- **Identity reuse is asserted across scenes and persistent elements.** Repeated requirements with
  one `identityKey` trigger one provider job and resolve to one digest. Unkeyed one-off requirements
  remain independent.
- **Candidate promotion is tested through the resolver interface.** Pending, rejected, malformed,
  and failed candidates never become `ready`. Acceptance binds the exact digest and makes the next
  compile quiet the corresponding placeholder finding.
- **Spend callbacks are fail-closed.** Missing, expired, mismatched, or already-consumed grants
  prevent the tool call rather than warning after it. Test call counts prove that the provider was
  not reached.
- **Long-running recovery is tested at the job seam.** A caller disconnects after job creation,
  resumes by job identifier, observes the original completion, and does not create a second job.
- **Phase ordering and concurrency are observable.** Research completes before narrative and art
  direction start; narrative and art direction may overlap; visual planning starts only after both;
  image work starts only after a validated requirement exists; Production cannot bind unaccepted
  candidate bytes.
- **Progress events are schema-validated and leak-scanned.** Tests assert their order and content,
  and reject secrets, repository locations, model reasoning, and raw binary data.
- **Session recovery is tested with a replaceable session implementation.** The crew is interrupted
  after Research or image-job creation, reconstructed with the same session and Production Run, and
  resumes without repeating completed external calls.
- **Existing regression suites remain authoritative for their seams.** Production-client parity,
  command lifecycle, Run ledger integrity, catalog freshness, compile validation, render purity,
  recorded stills, evidence verification, and cloud transport behavior stay green without being
  duplicated in crew tests.
- **Live tests are explicit operator runs.** One live Parallel tracer bullet and one live image
  generation are recorded with provider model/version, call counts, duration, accepted or rejected
  outcome, and cost-relevant usage. They never run in ordinary CI.
- **The final acceptance run is watched as well as checked.** Machine checks establish contracts,
  provenance, integrity, and completion. A human acceptance establishes that the generated image is
  editorially suitable and that the final preview is visually presentable.

## Out of Scope

- An autonomous general Quality Agent or an open-ended compiler-to-agent patch loop.
- An LLM Director with permission to improvise the production command sequence.
- Dynamic research swarms or one ADK agent per source.
- Multiple image candidates, tournament judging, or repeated autonomous regeneration in the first
  slice.
- Licensed-image search, stock-provider integration, advanced cutout, background removal, duotone
  processing, or a complete media asset pipeline.
- Generative video clips or replacement of deterministic Remotion rendering.
- A new SceneCapability, a hand-written scene for the demonstration, or changes to the six rules.
- MCP for the internal scene catalog.
- A second crew-specific copy of the catalog, check vocabulary, production commands, or asset
  worklist.
- Provider names, credentials, model identifiers, and provider response shapes in domain contracts.
- Public ingress to the Production service, a replacement cloud topology, or changes to the local
  and remote transport decisions.
- Replacing the Run store, Run ledger, command receipts, or production-client seam.
- Automatic canonical-library promotion. Repository assets still require deliberate human
  acceptance and a separate commit.
- Full Studio editing, prompt-based scene replacement, partial recompilation, collaboration,
  multi-user authorization, billing, or marketplace work.
- The formal ten-Brief Measurement gate. Its results remain a separate catalog measurement, though
  the tracer bullet must not make that future run harder.
- Editing the frozen architecture document.

## Further Notes

The detailed Director, Research, Narrative, Visual Planner, and Asset Resolver proposal comes from
the V1 product requirements. The frozen architecture supplies the stronger constraints: Visual
Planner catalog literacy, semantic time, the Asset Resolver's pipeline position, immediate honest
placeholders, `identityKey`, and the refusal to build a Quality Agent before the demonstration.

Current ADK documentation supports the chosen orchestration mechanisms: sequential and parallel
workflow agents, specialist model agents, shared session state through named outputs, callbacks that
can block tool execution, asynchronous runners, and long-running function tools. Framework features
are used where they remove custom orchestration, but the domain state machine remains explicit and
testable outside framework object structure.

This spec intentionally exposes two ADR changes instead of burying them in implementation:

- ADR-0017 must be superseded in its whole-catalog-author half before the Visual Planner role split
  lands. The measured twelve-capability wall and role authority, not token cost alone, are the
  argument.
- ADR-0005 must be extended before a generated asset can live only inside a Run. Its deterministic
  prompt, `identityKey`, warning worklist, human acceptance, and no-generation-during-render rules
  remain. The changed decision is how an accepted per-Run asset is persisted and reproduced.

Recommended implementation slices are:

1. Record the two successor ADR decisions and define the crew outcome/event contracts.
2. Make ADK execution asynchronous and the session implementation configurable while preserving the
   existing CLI adapter.
3. Integrate the Research Agent with live and recorded Parallel adapters and produce the Research
   dossier.
4. Add the Narrative Agent and Art Director parallel workflow with validated outputs.
5. Split visual planning into structure and SceneInstance authoring using generated catalog tiers,
   then integrate bounded refusal repair.
6. Extend the Asset Resolver with candidate lifecycle and add the Image Creator plus live and
   recorded generation adapters.
7. Add the published Production operations needed to start, observe, accept, and retrieve generated
   assets without revealing storage locations.
8. Drive the recorded whole-crew acceptance suite, then perform one deliberately authorized live
   tracer-bullet Run and preserve its evidence.

The feature is complete when the live tracer bullet begins with one factual Brief and ends with a
digest-verified narrated preview containing at least one human-accepted generated image, while its
Research dossier proves a real Parallel call, its VideoPlan remains code-blind and semantic, its
provider and production spend are explicitly authorized, and a dropped caller can resume without
repeating completed paid work.
