Scoped to packages/video — the hot spot across the last 60 commits. packages/production was checked and is not a candidate; see Checked, and fine.

Almost every module here is already deep. Two kinds of friction remain, and they are not module size:

  • The catalog publishes claims to the agent, and the code proving those claims is hand-maintained.
  • Rules that belong to no capability are living in four capabilities at once — under two different names.

Top recommendation · revised

4 · One render harness behind one interface

It is the only candidate an adversarial pass could not dent. Six suites each carry the same bundle-and-browser lifecycle; two of those copies are byte-identical. Collapsing them behind one call is a pure deepening with no ADR to argue with, no published contract to move, and no fixture to keep honest.

It is also the prerequisite for candidate 6, the mandatory suite ADR-0003 already accepted. Build the harness and the stress matrix inherits it instead of becoming a seventh copy.

Why not candidate 1 any more

It is still a real hole — bar_chart's claim is unproven and the compiler acts on it. But its cost was understated: deriving the capability list does not derive the ceiling fixtures, and the props must come from the schemas to stay honest. The history cited for ranking it first was also misread — the guard never failed. Do it second, sized properly.
